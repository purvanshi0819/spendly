Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects the information you provide when you create an account and use the Service, such as your name, email address, and the expense entries you record (amount, date, category, and description). We may also collect basic usage data like page visits to improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide and improve the expense tracking features of Spendly, to manage your account, and to communicate with you about your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use your financial entries for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Access is restricted to the account owner. You can delete your data at any time by removing individual expenses or deleting your account entirely, which permanently removes your records from our system.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party service providers (such as hosting and email delivery) to operate the Service. These providers only access your data as necessary to perform their functions and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the support channel associated with your Spendly account.
